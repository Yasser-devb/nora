# Nora Name and Branding Policy

**Status: proposed policy for maintainer review.** This document does not
establish that Nora, Morave Technologies, or any logo is a registered or
unregistered trademark, identify a legal owner, or provide legal advice. The
project owner should verify name/logo rights and any registration before
making ownership claims or enforcing a trademark policy.

## Project identity

The project identifies Nora as a framework developed in Morocco by Morave
Technologies. The project designates
[`Yasser-devb/nora`](https://github.com/Yasser-devb/nora) as its official
repository. Only releases published there by an authorized maintainer are
official Nora releases.

## Forks and modified distributions

The project license, once approved and published, governs permitted use,
modification, and redistribution of covered code. A permissive code license
such as MIT does not make a modified fork an official release, does not imply
endorsement by Morave Technologies, and does not by itself grant rights to
third-party marks or logos.

Fork maintainers should identify their distribution as modified or unofficial,
state their own maintainer and source repository, and avoid using project
branding in a way that suggests official status or endorsement. They may
accurately identify compatibility or lineage (for example, "a modified fork
of Nora") subject to applicable law and any rights that are ultimately
verified. Do not remove required copyright/license notices from code.

## Name and logo

Until ownership and usage terms are verified by the project owner:

- Do not claim that a Nora name or logo is registered or owned by a particular
  person or entity.
- Do not use any logo unless its source and permission are clear.
- Use "Nora" to identify the project or describe compatibility truthfully, not
  to imply endorsement, sponsorship, or official distribution of a fork.
- Authorized maintainers should approve project logos, visual identity, and
  any future trademark guidelines before publication.

The owner should review this policy, confirm any relevant rights, and decide
whether a formal trademark policy or registration is appropriate before a
public launch.

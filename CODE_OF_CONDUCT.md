# Community Code of Conduct

Nora is created and maintained by Morave Technologies in Morocco. We want
people with different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and
perspectives to be able to participate respectfully.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful in issues, reviews, and
  community spaces.
- Discuss ideas and behavior rather than attacking people.
- Accept good-faith correction and give actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information or
  private security report.
- Keep criticism proportionate and relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sustained
personal attacks, sexualized conduct without consent, doxxing, and repeated
disruption after a clear request to stop are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through a
verified private channel. No dedicated conduct-reporting address is configured
yet; the maintainer must publish a monitored channel before a broad public
community launch. Do not use public issues for sensitive personal information.

The authorized maintainer will review reports discreetly, hear from involved
people where appropriate, and decide a proportionate response. Actions may
include a private warning, removal of harmful content, temporary participation
limits, or removal from project spaces. Decisions about project spaces and
contribution acceptance remain with authorized maintainers; this does not
grant repository access or merge rights. The maintainer should explain
decisions to affected people while respecting confidentiality.

This is a project-specific policy, not legal advice. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for participation and licensing status.
